# Reference Inspection Notes

## Landing tiles 1–2

- The landing reference uses a near-black page background and a narrow centered content rail, approximately four portrait cards wide.
- The primary directory cards are tall portraits with a radius of roughly 22–26px, a full-bleed photographic image, and a strong black bottom gradient for copy legibility.
- Title text is bold white, positioned low on every card; an age sits inline in a softer gray-white weight. The descriptive line is short, muted, and clipped.
- Gutters are compact and consistent, while row spacing is visibly larger than the horizontal gap.
- A previous row enters at the top edge before the main rows, confirming a continuous masonry-like card catalog rather than isolated panels.
- A slim status-style pill can sit at a card’s upper right. Accent use is restrained, generally a pink-red outline or label against dark surfaces.

## Landing tiles 3–4

The card catalog keeps a four-column arrangement without tile-size variation. Every visual has a gently rounded container, a large editorial-style portrait, and the same lower third text treatment. The key fidelity signal is the disciplined repetition: large photographs, moderate row gaps, tiny outer background margins, and low-contrast charcoal gutters. There is no heavy border around each card; visual separation comes from the black background and the rounded image edges.

## Landing tiles 5–6

After the final directory row, a wide, low promotional panel spans the content rail. It uses a deeper wine-on-black graphical texture, an image group anchored left, a large white heading with a warm pink emphasis, concise explanatory copy, and a bright rounded pink action button at far right. A generous gap follows. The FAQ then has a centered high-contrast heading and full-width dark accordion rows with thin gray borders, large left-aligned prompts, and a small chevron at the far right.

## Landing tiles 7–8

The FAQ remains fully closed throughout, with 24–32px vertical separation between rows. After another broad dark gap, the lower-page information section is a rectangular charcoal panel with a faint border and modest rounded corners. It uses a large left-aligned headline with a pink highlighted phrase, followed by multiple comfortable paragraphs in subdued gray-white. The writing is not needed for fidelity; the visual density and text hierarchy are.

## Landing tile 9 and discover tile 1

The landing footer is a low, wide dark-gradient region with the wordmark at the left, a compact language button, three link columns, and a cookie-consent card overlaying the lower-right. The consent panel is black with a 1px gray outline, a short heading and message, a full-width secondary action, and paired reject/accept buttons; the accept control carries the site pink.

The in-app discovery reference instead uses a full-width app shell. A 62px top bar anchors the brand at left, category links in the middle, and account actions at right. A dark 220px left sidebar starts below the header, leaving a main column with a wide banner, a prominent section heading, and a horizontally scrollable row of tall promotional tiles. The main page has larger, more compact desktop content scale than the standalone landing view.

## Discover tiles 2–3

Below the promotion row, the browse controls form a clipped horizontal pill strip: a search field, a pink-outlined active choice, and several charcoal topic chips. The catalogue itself is a strict three-column grid at this viewport. Portrait cards are closer to a 0.7 width-to-height proportion, with ~20px radii, a lower black gradient, strong white title text, muted two-line summaries, and an occasional small violet play button. Status chips are small warm-pink capsules in the upper-right corner; special promotional cards use the same frame but add bright pink strokes and an oversized action button along the bottom.

## Discover tiles 4–5

The lower catalogue uses the exact same three-column card geometry and surface treatment, creating a long uninterrupted browsing canvas. The sidebar’s secondary utility items appear only near its lower edge and are separated from the primary navigation by vertical open space. The cookie panel is fixed in the bottom-right of the viewport, translucent charcoal with strong backdrop blur, a rounded 24px perimeter, and a small globe-like settings icon. Its paired actions are visually equal in size, but the accept choice uses a saturated pink fill. These mechanics will be reproduced as local, non-networked interactions and generalized adult-profile copy rather than copied source content.
